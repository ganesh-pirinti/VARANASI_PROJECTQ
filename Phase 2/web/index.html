PROBLEM STATEMENT & MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

The PS-O1 Network Partitioning Challenge

Bridging combinatorial graph theory, QUBO formulations, and hardware-aware quantum circuits.

1. Dual-Channel Max-Cut Formulation

Given graph $G = (V, E, w)$ with 6 nodes and 9 weighted links totaling weight 27, we assign each node to Group $S$ ($x_i=1$) or Group $\bar{S}$ ($x_i=0$). The weighted cut objective to maximize is:

C(x) = ∑_{(u,v) ∈ E} w_{uv} [x_u(1 - x_v) + x_v(1 - x_u)]

Separating crossing links achieves 81.48% (22/27) weighted cut score on the synthetic benchmark.

2. QUBO & Ising Spin Hamiltonian

Minimizing $-C(x) = x^T Q x$ with diagonal $Q_{ii} = -\text{deg}_w(i)$ and upper-triangular $Q_{ij} = 2 w_{ij}$. Mapping $x_i = (I - Z_i)/2$ yields the cost Hamiltonian:

H_min = ∑_{(u,v) ∈ E} (w_{uv} / 2) Z_u Z_v - (27 / 2) I

Implemented with basis gate block $CX(u,v) \to R_Z(\gamma \cdot w_{uv}) \to CX(u,v)$ using positive angle $\theta = \gamma w_{uv}$.
